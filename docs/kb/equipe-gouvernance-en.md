# FAME's team and governance

Most members are students recruited directly by the project's leads. They usually start with an internship, whose goal is to advance the project while working on subjects they enjoy. This is one reason the range of research subjects is fairly broad.

## Who leads FAME?

The project is led in both labs:
- Paris: Fabrice Riva and Serge Darolles.
- Montreal: David Ardia.

## How is the team organised between Paris and Montreal?

The two labs collaborate on the project while remaining independent: each can work on different subjects. Both teams are skilled in finance and machine learning (ML), with an emphasis: Montreal leans toward ML, Paris toward finance.

## What are the roles in the team?

All members work on the project at the same level; the main difference is how much time each can devote to it.

## Is FAME affiliated with an institution or partners?

FAME is a cross-university project bringing together Paris-Dauphine and HEC Montreal. Experiments also rely on a partner platform: Nextwise.

## How do I contact the FAME team?

Each member is listed on the "Team" page together with their email address, so you can contact the relevant person directly. For a general request or to suggest a topic, use the "Propose" page. You can also reach out to the project leads: Fabrice Riva or Serge Darolles in Paris, David Ardia in Montreal.

## Is FAME hiring (internships, PhDs, positions)?

It is possible to join the project; to do so, contact the leads directly. Most members started with an internship.
