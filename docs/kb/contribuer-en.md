# Contributing to FAME

## How do I propose a research topic?

Anyone, visitors included, can propose a topic via the "Propose" page; the team then reviews the proposal. Propose a topic if you think it might interest the project, that it deserves to be studied, or that one of our subjects would benefit from a different angle. To catch our attention, the proposal should relate to the project; if you are unsure what we do, browse the site or ask the assistant. Proposals are handled as quickly as possible, and you will get a reply if you provide your email address in the form. A topic may be declined.

## How do I become a FAME member?

There is no open sign-up: an administrator invites members (via an activation email). To learn more about what is possible, you can contact the project leads — Serge Darolles or Fabrice Riva. Most members started with an internship.

## What can a member do that a visitor cannot?

A signed-in member can create and edit subjects, and manage tasks, publications and datasets. A visitor can read public content, comment, and propose subjects.

## How does work on a subject unfold?

When a subject emerges, it is broken down into tasks to carry out. Members freely take on the tasks that interest them, all under the oversight of the project leads. Progress is tracked on each subject's task board.

## Can I collaborate with FAME or reuse your work?

To find out whether collaboration or reuse of our work is possible, please contact one of the project leads.
