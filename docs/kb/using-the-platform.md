# Using the platform

## Home and choosing a lab

The home page shows an interactive globe with pins for Paris and Montreal. Click a lab to enter its space; navigation then stays within that lab. A language switcher (English / French) is available on every page.

## Research subjects

Each lab presents its research subjects as readable sheets. A subject sheet gathers its description, the tasks tracking its progress, related files and comments. Visitors can read public subjects; members can add and edit them.

## Tasks

Subjects are broken down into tasks shown on a board, with a status and assignees, so the team can coordinate progress.

## Publications

The Publications page lists the lab's papers and outputs.

## Team

The Team page is a directory of the lab's members, including each member's email address, so you can contact a researcher directly.

## Data and Prompts

The Data page (datasets linked from Dropbox) and the Prompts page are reserved for signed-in members.

## Proposing a subject

Anyone can suggest a research topic from the Propose page. Proposals are reviewed by the team before they become research subjects.
