# FAQ

## What is FAME?

FAME is a research initiative run by two independent labs, in Paris and Montreal. Its research focuses on AI-driven sentiment signals from financial news, with an empirical scope centered on Euronext.

## What does FAME research?

FAME investigates signals built from the sentiment of financial news, analysed with AI and machine-learning techniques, and how those signals relate to markets — primarily Euronext-listed instruments.

## Can I propose a research topic?

Yes. Use the "Propose" page to submit a subject. The team reviews proposals before they become research subjects.

## How do I contact a researcher?

Individual contact details are not published on the site. Use the Propose page to reach the team.

## What is the difference between a visitor and a member?

Visitors can read public content, comment, and propose subjects. Members contribute actively: they create and edit subjects, manage tasks, publications and datasets. Membership is granted by an administrator — there is no self sign-up.

## Are the Paris and Montreal labs separate?

Yes. The two labs are independent: each has its own subjects, tasks, members, publications and datasets. You choose a lab from the home page and navigate within that context.

## In which language does the assistant answer?

The site is available in English and French, and the assistant replies in the language of your question.
