# FAQ — additional questions

## Is FAME a company, a university lab, or a research project?

FAME is a cross-university research project, run by Paris-Dauphine and HEC Montreal.

## How long has FAME existed?

The project was launched in 2024.

## Can the Astra assistant be wrong?

Yes. Astra relies on FAME's content and may make mistakes or miss recent information; for any important decision, check with the team. Astra does not invent facts: if it does not know, it says so and invites you to propose the topic.

## Are my questions to the assistant stored?

Questions may be logged to improve the assistant, without your identity; your IP address is only kept as a salted hash (not reversible in practice) and is not used to identify you. See the Privacy page.

## In which languages can I use the site and the assistant?

The site is available in English (default) and French. The assistant replies in the language of your question.

## How do I report an error or send feedback?

Use the "Report a problem" button, available at the bottom of the page next to the Privacy link: it opens a short form that sends your message to the team. You can also use the "Propose" page.

## Can the assistant access confidential data?

No, not for a visitor: the assistant never returns a confidential subject or member-only data. Member email addresses, however, are public (on the "Team" page) and may be shared. A signed-in member can see all member-only material.
