# Data and publications

## What data does FAME use, and where does it come from?

The signals are built from financial news drawn from the specialised European press. These texts are analysed to extract a sentiment, which then feeds the construction of market signals. The reference market scope is Euronext.

## How do I access the datasets?

The data explorer (the "Data" page, backed by Dropbox) is reserved for signed-in members. Visitors do not have access to it.

## What types of publications does FAME produce?

FAME produces various scientific outputs: articles, preprints, working papers, and conference talks. Individual publications are listed on the platform, and the assistant can enumerate them.

## Where can I find FAME's publications?

On each lab's "Publications" page.

## Is the data and are the results public or restricted?

Some content is public: the publications and the descriptions of research subjects. Other items are reserved for members: raw data (the "Data" page) and subjects marked as confidential. The assistant never discloses member-only content to a visitor.
