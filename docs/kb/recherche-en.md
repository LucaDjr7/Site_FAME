# FAME's research programme

## What is FAME's scientific goal?

FAME builds and evaluates market signals derived from the sentiment of financial news. The core idea: automatically analyse financial news to extract a tone (sentiment), turn it into quantitative signals, and study how those signals relate to market behaviour. The empirical scope is the Euronext markets.

## What data does FAME work with?

The signals are built from financial news drawn from the specialised European press. On the market side, the reference scope is Euronext, the pan-European exchange; current work focuses on the Oslo and Lisbon Euronext universes.

## What methods and techniques do you use?

The methodological core combines natural language processing (NLP) — including large language models (LLMs) — to estimate the sentiment of texts, with quantitative methods from finance and machine learning to build and evaluate the signals. Evaluation relies notably on backtests over historical data. The two labs are complementary: Montreal leans toward machine learning, Paris toward finance.

## What is a "sentiment signal" at FAME?

It is a quantitative indicator derived from the sentiment of financial news, meant to be related to market behaviour. Concretely, we start from the news, estimate its tone with NLP methods, then aggregate that information into a usable signal for market analysis.

## What makes FAME's approach distinctive?

FAME is a cross-university project bringing together Paris-Dauphine and HEC Montreal, combining a dual expertise in finance and machine learning. Experiments also rely on a partner platform, Nextwise. This combination — European financial press, AI/NLP methods, Euronext scope, and Paris–Montreal collaboration — is the project's signature.

## What are the research areas?

The range of topics is deliberately broad: members (often interns) choose subjects they enjoy, which naturally widens the field explored around the shared theme (sentiment of financial news and market signals).
