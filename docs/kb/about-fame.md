# About FAME

FAME is a research initiative run by two independent labs, in Paris and Montreal. Its research focuses on AI-driven sentiment signals extracted from financial news, with an empirical scope centered on Euronext markets.

## Research focus

FAME studies how the sentiment of financial news — analysed with AI and machine-learning methods — can be turned into signals, and how those signals relate to market behaviour. The empirical scope is centered on Euronext, the pan-European exchange. Typical work includes building news-sentiment signals, evaluating them against market data, and documenting the findings as research subjects and publications.

## The two labs

FAME is organised as two independent labs, in Paris and Montreal. Each lab keeps its own research subjects, tasks, members, publications and datasets. A member belongs to a lab, and can belong to both when needed. The two labs are separate spaces: you choose one from the home page and navigate within that context.

## What this platform offers

This site is both an internal tool for the team and a public showcase. Visitors can read about ongoing research subjects, browse publications, meet the team, and propose new research topics. Members contribute actively: they create and update subjects, track tasks, and manage publications and datasets.
